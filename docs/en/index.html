Diagnosis of hardwareobsolescenciaand migration, guided by AI.

MIGRA-IA is anasistidoconversational agent that guides technical staff step by step in diagnosing the obsolescence of PLCs, I/O modules, HMIs, industrial networks and drives, and in planning their migration — with traceability, confidence levels and mandatory human validation.

Cuestionario adaptativo

It captures the equipment information section by section; the answers enable, hide or modify the questions that follow, with a confidence level per item.

0–100

Obsolescence risk

Eight weighted factors produce a deterministic score and its classification. Same case, same score — auditable.

ID + audit trail

Expediente trazable

Every asset, piece of evidence and decision receives a unique identifier and is left in an audit trail that can be reconstructed.

Section 8

Safety rules

It does not invent catalog numbers, does not assume compatibility or backups, marks missing data and demands human approval before intervening.

Consulting

It steers the solution

Compared alternatives, equivalences suggested on technical criteria (to be verified with the manufacturer) and a staged migration procedure.

Section 9

Technical report

It automatically generates a traceable report that separates confirmed facts, inferences and recommendations, with their sources.

State of the project — honest and verifiable

MIGRA-IA is aprototipo (v0.4.0). The code and its documentation are public and reproducible, and the artifact isarchived in Zenodo with DOI (10.5281/zenodo.21480949). Publishing the artifactdoes not require an API key; the key only enables natural language reasoning over arbitrary data (a paid Anthropic service; each user provides their own). Scope of the MVP: source Siemens S7-300/400, target S7-1500, STEP 7 Classic and TIA Portal, MPI/Profibus DP/Profinet/Ethernet networks.

Aviso.A technical assistance system. It does not replace functional safety assessments, lockout and tagout procedures, plant standards or the authorization of competent personnel. Always verify the recommendations before intervening on real equipment.

The hardware equivalences and families mentioned are indicative and must be verified with the manufacturer's official selection or migration tool. MIGRA-IA never confirms catalog numbers, part numbers or nameplate data.
