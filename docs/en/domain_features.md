# Domain features

Generated by `_features.py` reading `data/en/questionnaire.json` and
`docs/en/scoring_rules.md`, which is itself extracted from the syntax
tree of the engine. If a factor stops reading a code, this document changes
by itself.

Box 3 of the deliverable. The decision is taken and it is this one: **we do
not go with the raw data**. The project defines domain features -engineering
knowledge turned into a number- and weighs them; what follows says which
ones, with what weight, and which ones are collected and not used.

## The candidates of the field, against what the engine does

| Feature | Codes | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Years since release | C05, D07 | **Asked and NOT used** (C05, D07) |
| End of support | M03, M02 | **Asked and NOT used** (M03, M02) |
| Status in the manufacturer's catalog | M01 | **Used** by the engine (M01) |
| Spare parts | M04, M05, M06 | **Used** by the engine (M04, M06); M05 are asked and not used |
| Protocol | G01 | **Used** by the engine (G01) |
| Criticality | C08, C10, Q01 | **Used** by the engine (C08, C10); Q01 are asked and not used |

**Years since release.** Year of manufacture and of installation. It is on the nameplate of the equipment and it predates any end-of-life event, so it cannot be contaminated by the label.

**End of support.** Year of end of support and of end of sale declared by the manufacturer. Both optional in the questionnaire: they are asked for 'if known'.

**Status in the manufacturer's catalog.** The one the domain calls for: it is the variable a plant manager already uses to decide.

**Spare parts.** Availability of a new spare part, of the secondary market and the lead time.

**Protocol.** Networks the machine uses. It determines how much the change drags along.

**Criticality.** Criticality of the machine, tolerable downtime and the cost of an hour stopped.

## Gaps: what is collected and thrown away

The questionnaire asks the technician for these data, stores them in the
case file and **no factor reads them**. It is the same class of defect as
F12, but on the two features the field names first:

- `C05` — Approximate year of manufacture  \[Years since release\]
- `D07` — Approximate year of installation  \[Years since release\]
- `M03` — Year in which technical support and the supply of spare parts end, if known  \[End of support\]
- `M02` — Year in which the manufacturer stopped (or will stop) selling the product, if known  \[End of support\]
- `M05` — Are used or refurbished spare parts available on the secondary market?  \[Spare parts\]
- `Q01` — Approximate cost of one hour of downtime of this machine, stating the currency  \[Criticality\]

Closing it means touching `scoring.py`, whose eight factors and weights are
the ones the paper describes. It is an editorial decision, not a technical
one, and it is recorded as such.

## In the life-cycle table only one survives

The above is the questionnaire, which is where the domain knowledge of the
project lives. The tabular set on which the P1 baseline is trained is a
different one -`data/platform_lifecycle.csv`, nine platforms- and there the
leakage audit leaves **a single admissible variable: the age**. It is not a
modelling choice: the other columns either define the label or are metadata
of the collection process. See section 6 of
`docs/en/reproducible_baseline.md`.

Hence why enlarging the set is the task that unblocks the rest: as long
as the table has nine rows and one variable, the domain features are
defined but they cannot be exercised.
